# Idle AI Empire — Vision

---

# Current Milestone (PoC — 2026-05-16)

> "Would someone voluntarily keep playing this?"

**Status:** Core gameplay loop is built. Phases 1–4 complete.
Next step: play-test and balance (Phase 5).

---

# Core Fantasy

The player starts with a weak AI setup in a garage.

Over time they evolve into the owner of the largest compute empire in existence.

The fantasy is:
- infinite scale
- technological acceleration
- automation
- exponential growth
- becoming absurdly powerful

The player should constantly feel:
> "My empire is growing faster and faster."

---

# Pillars

## 1. Satisfying Progression

Every interaction should:
- increase numbers
- unlock systems
- create momentum
- reward optimisation

The game should avoid:
- dead time
- confusing mechanics
- excessive friction

---

## 2. Strong Theme

This is NOT a generic clicker game.

The theme should feel:
- modern
- tech-focused
- AI-inspired
- slightly satirical
- visually futuristic

Possible tone:
- corporate AI absurdity
- startup culture parody
- runaway technological growth

---

## 3. Layered Growth

Progression should evolve in layers.

### Early Game (PoC scope)
- GPUs
- small racks
- manual clicking
- cheap upgrades

### Mid Game (future)
- data centres
- cooling systems
- automation
- AI agents
- research

### Late Game (future)
- orbital compute
- quantum chips
- autonomous AI economies
- planetary infrastructure

---

# Long-Term Systems

Potential future systems:

- Prestige / Singularity resets
- Research trees
- Achievements
- Daily rewards
- Events
- Limited-time boosts
- Cosmetic upgrades
- Multiple resource types
- Rare discoveries
- Global leaderboard (optional)

---

# Monetisation Philosophy

> Not relevant until gameplay is validated.

Monetisation should:
- enhance progression
- never feel hostile
- respect player time

Preferred:
- rewarded ads
- optional boosts
- cosmetic purchases
- remove ads purchase

Avoid:
- aggressive interstitials
- paywalls
- energy systems
- predatory mechanics

---

# Visual Direction

Style:
- dark UI
- neon accents (cyan primary, purple secondary)
- glowing stats
- futuristic dashboards
- clean monospaced typography

Inspirations:
- sci-fi control rooms
- AI server racks
- cyberpunk interfaces
- modern SaaS dashboards

---

# Success Criteria

The project succeeds if:

1. The game is satisfying to play repeatedly
2. Players return daily
3. Session length increases over time
4. Monetisation feels natural
5. The game can scale with additional content

# Idle AI Empire — Vision

---

# Current Milestone (2026-05-17)

**Status:** Phases 1–11 complete.

What's built:
- 12 upgrade tiers (GPU → Planetary Grid)
- 10 research nodes with permanent multipliers (Compute cost, linear unlock)
- Auto-Tapper (25 GPUs → 1 passive tap/sec, pulsing ring UI)
- Prestige / Singularity (reset at 1T, shard multiplier ×1.1^shards)
- 15 achievements with toast notifications and AWARDS screen
- Offline earnings on cold launch and foreground return (up to 16h)
- UPGRADES / RESEARCH / AWARDS tab UI

Next: Phase 12 (Sound & Visual Polish), then Phase 13 (Monetisation Foundation).

---

# Core Fantasy

The player starts with a weak AI setup in a garage.

Over time they evolve into the owner of the largest compute empire in existence.

The fantasy is:
- infinite scale
- technological acceleration
- automation
- exponential growth
- becoming absurdly powerful

The player should constantly feel:
> "My empire is growing faster and faster."

---

# Pillars

## 1. Satisfying Progression

Every interaction should:
- increase numbers
- unlock systems
- create momentum
- reward optimisation

The game should avoid:
- dead time
- confusing mechanics
- excessive friction

---

## 2. Strong Theme

This is NOT a generic clicker game.

The theme should feel:
- modern
- tech-focused
- AI-inspired
- slightly satirical
- visually futuristic

Possible tone:
- corporate AI absurdity
- startup culture parody
- runaway technological growth

---

## 3. Layered Growth

Progression should evolve in layers.

### Early Game ✅
- GPUs, Server Racks, Data Centres
- manual tapping
- research unlocks early multipliers

### Mid Game ✅
- AI Clusters, Hyperscalers, Quantum Cores
- Cooling Arrays, AI Agents, Neural Fabric
- research tree with CPS + tap multipliers
- Auto-Tapper milestone

### Late Game ✅
- Orbital Stations, Dyson Swarms, Planetary Grid
- top research nodes: Autonomous Replication → Planetary Consciousness
- Prestige / Singularity resets with shard multiplier

### Systems still to build
- Sound effects (Phase 12)
- Visual polish — unlock animations, milestone fanfare (Phase 12)
- Monetisation (Phase 13)

# Monetisation Philosophy

Phase 10 is complete — monetisation can now be planned and built (Phase 13).

Monetisation should:
- enhance progression
- never feel hostile
- respect player time

Preferred:
- rewarded ads (×2 compute for 30s)
- optional boosts
- cosmetic purchases
- remove ads purchase

Avoid:
- aggressive interstitials
- paywalls
- energy systems
- predatory mechanics

# Success Criteria

The project succeeds if:

1. The game is satisfying to play repeatedly
2. Players return daily
3. Session length increases over time
4. Monetisation feels natural
5. The game can scale with additional content
